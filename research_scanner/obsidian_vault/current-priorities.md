# Strategic Research Priorities

1. **Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing & Error Correction**: Focus on recent breakthroughs in topological color codes (`Spacetime Layout and Logical Compilation of Color Code`), stabilizer qubit loss inference, and VQE quantum architecture search (`DreamQAS`). Target hardware-software compilation bottlenecks.

2. **Autonomous LLM Agents & Sequential Decision Making**: Evaluate experimental benchmarks for multi-agent topology adaptation (`MANTA`), automated hyperparameter optimization (`AgentHPOBench`), and agentic self-correction tool-flows (`AMTFV`). Focus on practical execution and reliability.

3. **Physical Robotics & Humanoid Safety Control**: Track vision-language-action (VLA) models, reactive diffusion policies (`FA-RDP`), and control barrier function reinforcement learning (`PAC-MAN`) for humanoid balance and contact-rich manipulation.
