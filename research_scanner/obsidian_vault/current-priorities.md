# Strategic Research Priorities

1. **Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing & Error Correction**: Focus on topological color codes, stabilizer qubit loss inference, and decision-useful world models for VQE quantum architecture search (`DreamQAS`). Target hardware-software compilation bottlenecks.

2. **Autonomous LLM Agents & Sequential Decision Making**: Evaluate experimental benchmarks for long-horizon agentic runtimes (`Argus`), multi-agent topology adaptation (`MANTA`), automated HPO (`AgentHPOBench`), and agentic self-correction tool-flows (`AMTFV`).

3. **Physical Robotics & Humanoid Safety Control**: Track vision-language-action (VLA) models (`BridgeVLA++`, `Gemini Robotics 2`), reactive diffusion policies (`FA-RDP`), and control barrier function reinforcement learning (`PAC-MAN`) for humanoid balance, safety verification, and contact-rich manipulation.
