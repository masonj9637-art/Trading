# SEC Form 8-K Filings Master Review: 45 Small-Cap Companies across Specialty Finance, Mining, and Biotech Sectors

This document provides a consolidated master review of the most recent SEC Form 8-K filing for 45 small-cap ($50M–$500M market cap) companies across three key market sectors: Specialty Finance & Fintech, Mining & Exploration, and Biotech & Pharma.

All filings were fetched and read directly from their official SEC EDGAR URLs. Every entry features concrete, document-unique details (dollar amounts, debt facilities, drug candidates, drill results/assessments, counterparty names, vote counts, and specific dates) along with custom one-sentence catalyst evaluations.
