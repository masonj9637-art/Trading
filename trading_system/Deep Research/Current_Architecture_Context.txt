# Current AI Statistical Arbitrage Architecture

## 1. Core Paradigm
The system operates a Long/Short Dollar-Neutral Statistical Arbitrage strategy. It currently generates a +2.56% CAGR with a 0.97 Sharpe Ratio and an incredibly tight -3.36% Max Drawdown.

## 2. Data Pipeline & Alpha Isolation
1. **Raw Input**: We fetch Daily Close OHLCV data from Alpaca for S&P 500 constituents.
2. **Returns Conversion**: Prices are converted into percentage returns (`.pct_change()`).
3. **PCA Orthogonalization**: We use a 3-component Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to extract the systematic market beta from the returns. The residuals (idiosyncratic returns) are passed forward.
4. **EMA Smoothing**: We apply an Exponential Moving Average (EMA) with `alpha=0.3` to the residuals to remove high-frequency noise.
5. **Tokenization**: The smoothed residuals are formatted into an AutoGluon `TimeSeriesDataFrame`.

## 3. The AI Foundation Model (Signal Layer)
- We are currently using **Chronos-Bolt** (a fast, patch-based univariate foundation model).
- The model acts as a Bayesian expectation operator. We feed it a 100-day context window of smoothed residuals, and it forecasts the median (`0.5` quantile) expected residual return for the next day.
- It also generates the `0.1` and `0.9` quantile predictions, giving us a mathematically calibrated uncertainty fan.
- **Autoregressive Stripping**: We subtract the current moving average from the AI's forecast to isolate the pure predictive alpha signal.
- **Ranking**: The final alpha scores are cross-sectionally ranked and dollar-neutral normalized.

## 4. The Adaptive Core
- We built an `EnsembleAgent` powered by a **Thompson Sampling Multi-Armed Bandit** algorithm.
- It tracks the Bayesian probability of success (wins/losses) for various AI agents across different market regimes (Bull, Bear, Chop). 
- *Note: We recently fired our naive Momentum and Mean Reversion agents because they lost money. The Thompson Sampler currently allocates 100% of capital to Chronos, but the architecture is ready for new, advanced agents.*

## 5. The 12-Rule Governance Firewall
Before execution, signals must pass through a strict, deterministic firewall:
- **Max Drawdown Circuit Breaker**: Halts trading if the portfolio drops 10% from its peak.
- **Concentration Limit**: Caps max exposure to any single asset at 20% of the portfolio.
- **Max Gross Leverage**: Capped at 1.0x (unleveraged).
- **VaR Confidence Threshold**: Examines the distance between Chronos's `0.1` and `0.9` quantile bounds. If the uncertainty band exceeds a strict 5% (`var_limit=0.05`), the trade is exponentially scaled down or entirely vetoed.

## 6. Execution Layer
- Approved weights are translated into dollar allocations based on real-time Account NAV.
- We route asynchronous Bracket Orders via Alpaca. 
- Take Profit and Stop Loss limits are dynamically set exactly to the `0.1` and `0.9` quantile prediction bounds generated by Chronos.
